      # About this newsletter

      The *Louisa Condominium Newsletter* is how the Board of Directors keeps owners informed. After each board meeting we publish a short issue covering what was discussed, what was decided, and anything the board would like your input on.

      ## When the board needs your input

      Some issues include items marked **Your input needed**. When comments are open, you can share your thoughts right on the issue page. Please include your name and unit number. Comments are reviewed by the board before they appear publicly, and each item has a word limit and a deadline.

      ## Get each issue by email

      Use the subscribe form at the bottom of any page. You'll get one email each time a new issue is published, and every email includes an unsubscribe link.

      ## Printing

      Each issue has a **Print this issue** button that lays it out on a single letter-size page, ready for the lobby notice board.
